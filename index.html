How To Use
▦ Manual Tickers
Compare up to 5 stocks or ETFs side-by-side, with dividends reinvested (DRIP) and splits applied.
  1. Enter up to 5 tickers — e.g. AAPL, RY.TO, SPY.
  2. Click the coloured dot to change a line's colour.
  3. Pick a date range (preset or custom) and set your investment amount.
  4. Set tax on dividends — 0% for TFSA/RRSP, or 15–40% for taxable.
  5. Click ▶ Run Analysis.
⊞ ETF Holdings
Load any ETF and chart its top holdings individually — useful for seeing which stocks are driving performance.
  1. Click the ETF Holdings tab, type a ticker (e.g. QQQ), click Load Holdings.
  2. Check the holdings to chart. Optionally add up to 2 extra Compare Against tickers.
  3. Click ▶ Run Analysis.
Chart Options
  1. View: Total Return · Price Return · Div+Split · Raw Price  |  $ / % toggle.
  2. Indices: overlay S&P 500, Nasdaq, TSX, VIX, or BTC.
  3. Zoom: Ctrl+Scroll or pinch · drag to pan · +/− · Reset.
  4. ✂ Crop: drag on the chart to re-run from a zoomed window.
  5. Click a legend item to hide/show · click a ticker to open Yahoo Finance.
Settings save automatically. ✕ Clear resets everything.
